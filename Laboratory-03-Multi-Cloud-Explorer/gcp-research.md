## Three Advantages

1. **Strong Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning Capabilities** – Google Cloud provides powerful tools and infrastructure for developing, training, and deploying AI and machine learning applications.
2. **Kubernetes and Container Support** – Google Cloud offers Google Kubernetes Engine (GKE), a managed Kubernetes service that simplifies the deployment and management of containerized applications.
3. **High-Performance and Scalable Infrastructure** – Google Cloud provides scalable computing, storage, networking, and data services that can support demanding workloads.

## Typical Enterprise Use Cases

1. **Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning** – Organizations use Google Cloud services to develop, train, and deploy AI and machine learning applications.
2. **Data Analytics and Big Data** – Enterprises use Google Cloud's data and analytics services to process large datasets and gain useful business insights.
3. **Containerized Application Deployment** – Companies use Google Kubernetes Engine (GKE) to deploy, manage, and scale containerized applications across cloud infrastructure.
