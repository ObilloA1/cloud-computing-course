## Three Advantages

1. **Wide Range of Services** – AWS provides a large selection of cloud services for computing, storage, databases, networking, security, artificial intelligence, and more.
2. **High Scalability and Flexibility** – AWS allows organizations to easily increase or decrease their resources based on their workload and business requirements.
3. **Global Infrastructure** – AWS has a large global network of Regions and Availability Zones, helping organizations build reliable and highly available applications.

## Typical Enterprise Use Cases

1. **Web and Mobile Applications** – Enterprises use Amazon EC2, S3, and other AWS services to develop, host, and scale web and mobile applications.
2. **Data Backup and Disaster Recovery** – Organizations use Amazon S3 and other storage services to back up important business data and support disaster recovery.
3. **Enterprise Data and Business Applications** – Companies use AWS databases, analytics, networking, and computing services to run business systems and process large amounts of data.
